# View the SIM audit log

To display the settings for a SIM, on the SIM List menu, select ![](../../.gitbook/assets/infinityclassic-0062-SimActionIconAudit_24x24.png) next to the chosen SIM. This displays the SIM Audit Log page in a dialog.

[![](../../.gitbook/assets/infinityclassic-224-SimAuditLogDialog_thumb_400_400.png)](../../.gitbook/assets/infinityclassic-225-SimAuditLogDialog.png)

The SIM Audit Log displays a record of every action taken, and change made, to all SIMs associated with the selected SIM.
