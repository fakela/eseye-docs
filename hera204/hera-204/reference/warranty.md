* Do NOT open the casing under any circumstances, as it will invalidate the warranty. There are no user-serviceable components inside the Hera. If a fault develops with the product, please contact your service provider or reseller.
* Take all reasonable precautions when installing the Hera device. Read and understand all warnings and installation instructions. Eseye Design Ltd are not liable for consequential loss or damage that occurs should the warnings and installation instructions not be followed.
* Do not attempt to insert or remove a SIM card when the Hera 204 is powered on. This will cause problems with the Hera 204 functionality. Always power off before inserting or removing a SIM card.
