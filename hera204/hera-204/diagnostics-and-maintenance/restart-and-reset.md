# Restart and reset

### Reboot

In **Restart**, click **Reboot**. In the confirmation dialog, click **Yes** to restart the Hera 204.

### Factory reset

In **Restart**, click **Reset**. In the confirmation dialog, click **Yes** to restore factory settings.
