# Device Recovery

The following section describes available options for recovery of malfunctioning device. Should a device become unreachable due to power failure during firmware upgrade, or if its core files were wrongly modified in the file system, Eseye's routers offer several options for recovery.

## Reset button

Reset button is located on the back panel of the device. The reset button has two functions:

Reboot the device. After the device has powered up, the reset button can be pressed to reboot the device. Start of the reboot will be indicated by the Power LED turning solid red.

Reset to factory defaults. After the device has powered on, press the reset button for at least 5 seconds. The device will reset all user changes to factory defaults and reboot. Start of the factory reset will be indicated by the Power LED turning solid red.
