# Data volume and commercial model

Connectivity Metrics is a commercial service. The charge reflects the cost of generating, processing and delivering network data continuously across the estate.

### What drives volume

Data volumes depend on the size of your estate and how active it is. A large or busy deployment can produce significant volumes, particularly on the NetFlow side, where every flow within every session generates a record.

Because of that, pricing depends on the number of devices and the level of traffic rather than on a flat rate.

### Planning for it

Volume is worth estimating early, since it affects more than the commercial conversation. It also determines your retrieval cadence, your storage footprint and the cost of querying the data once it lands in your platform. Teams that treat the feed as an ordinary warehouse ingestion problem tend to have a much easier time than those that treat it as an occasional export.

### Starting with a trial

A short trial period is a common starting point. It lets you ingest real data from your own estate, confirm the volumes you will actually see, and validate the value of the datasets before committing to a broader rollout.
