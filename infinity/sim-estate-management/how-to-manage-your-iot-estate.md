# How to manage your IoT estate

We provide a comprehensive suite of tools and services designed to help you efficiently manage, monitor and organise your IoT estate, in order to make data-driven decisions and maintain peak operational efficiency. With features like real-time analytics, SIM lifecycle management, customisable alerts, and detailed billing insights, we offer everything you need to track your network usage, optimise performance, and maintain control over large-scale deployments.

Additionally, our platform supports back-office management functions for packages and portfolios, used for organizing and billing IoT services across customer accounts.

### Analytics

The Analytics section includes an overview of the dashboard, detailed reports, and live data streaming capabilities, which are tools that provide insights into your SIM and network usage. These features help you monitor SIM and network performance in real-time, analyse trends, and make data-driven decisions for optimal device management.
