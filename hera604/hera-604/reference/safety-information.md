# Safety information

## Safety information

Read these requirements before installing or using the Hera 604.

{% hint style="danger" %}
Never rely on cellular connectivity for life-critical communications.
{% endhint %}

### Installation environment

* Keep the router away from direct sunlight and heat sources.
* Do not enclose the router where heat can accumulate.
* Avoid dust, industrial residue, liquids, and airborne particles.

Install the router within one metre of mains power.

Mount the router no higher than two metres.

### Antennas and RF

Keep supplied antennas indoors.

Use lightning protection with any outdoor antenna.

Keep cellular antennas one metre from people during normal use.

Do not use the router where RF equipment is prohibited.

Do not use it on aircraft. Avoid flammable gases and blasting operations.

Check local rules in healthcare facilities. Test for interference before permanent installation.

### Power safety

Use an approved Limited Power Source adapter only.

The adapter must comply with IEC/UL 60950-1 or IEC/UL 62368-1.

Do not modify the mains adapter.
