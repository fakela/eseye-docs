# Compliance and disposal

## Compliance and disposal

The Hera 604 complies with these European requirements:

* RoHS Directive 2015/863/EU
* WEEE Directive 2012/19/EU
* Ecodesign Directive 2009/125/EC
* Batteries Directive 2013/56/EU
* Radio Equipment Directive 2014/53/EU
* Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU
* EMC Directive 2014/30/EU

The router carries the CE mark. The CE mark was affixed in 2024. It also carries FCC and ISED declarations.

Maintain at least 20 cm between the router antennas and people during operation. Use only approved antennas within their stated gain limits.

The router contains lithium coin cell batteries. Only manufacturer-authorised personnel may replace them.

At end of life, separate the router and accessories from other waste. Send them to your national WEEE collection system for recycling under EU Directive 2012/19/EC.
