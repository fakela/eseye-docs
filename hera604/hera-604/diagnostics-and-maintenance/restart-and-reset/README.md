# Restart and reset

#### Reboot

Open **Diagnostics & Maintenance > Restart > Reboot** and select **Reboot** to restart the router without intentionally erasing its saved configuration.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-08-14 at 16.59.59.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



Confirm the action in the displayed dialog. The router and its network services will be unavailable while it restarts. After reconnecting, check **System Status > Router** to confirm the new uptime and verify WAN connectivity.

#### Factory reset

Open **Diagnostics & Maintenance > Restart > Factory reset** and select **Reset** to return the router to its factory configuration.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-08-14 at 17.00.03.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



> **Warning:** A factory reset removes the operational configuration and may make the router unreachable at its previous address. Download a backup first and use factory reset only when the router must be rebuilt or Eseye Support instructs you to do so.

Before confirming a factory reset, ensure that you have:

* An approved configuration backup.
* The initial access details and expected default LAN address.
* The mobile, WAN, wireless, routing, firewall, and hosted-application settings needed to rebuild the router.
* On-site or direct local access.
* An approved outage window.
