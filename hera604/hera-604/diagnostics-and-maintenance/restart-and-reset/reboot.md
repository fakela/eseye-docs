# Reboot

Open **Diagnostics & Maintenance → Restart → Reboot**, then select **Reboot** to restart the router without intentionally erasing its saved configuration.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-08-14 at 16.59.59.png" alt="Restart page with the Reboot button."><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Confirm the action in the displayed dialog. The router and its network services are unavailable while it restarts.

After reconnecting, open **System Status → Router**. Confirm the new uptime and WAN connectivity.
