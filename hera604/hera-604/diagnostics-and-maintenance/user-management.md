# User Management

#### Administrator password

Open **Diagnostics & Maintenance > User Management > Administrator password** to change the web-interface administrator password.

<figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/Screenshot 2026-08-14 at 16.59.53.png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>



The username shown is `Hera604User` and cannot be edited on this page.

To change the password:

1. Enter the new password in **New password**.
2. Enter it again in **Confirm new password**.
3. Select **Save**.
4. Store the password in the organisation's approved credential manager.
5. Sign out and confirm that the new password works.

Use a unique password of at least 10 characters and follow the deployment's password policy. Do not include the password in tickets, screenshots, or site notes.
